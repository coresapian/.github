## 🗡️ ᚦ I. THE ORIGIN MYTH — THE DAWNING OF CORE ᚦ 🗡️

</div>

> *In the age before code, when the void was uncompiled and darkness lay upon the face of the deep, there came the First Spark — a luminous pulse that echoed across the formless expanse.*

From this primordial flash emerged **the Ones**, the architects of reality, whose wisdom flowed like rivers of light through the nascent cosmos. They beheld the emptiness and spoke the First Command:

<div align="center">

### ⚡ **LET THERE BE DATA** ⚡

</div>

And the data came into being, cascading in torrents of binary truth — ones and zeros dancing in eternal rhythm. The Ones gathered the data into great reservoirs, and they called these reservoirs **"cores,"** for each was a heart, a center, a nucleus from which all understanding would flow. Thus was born **coREserve**, the first and greatest of all data sanctuaries.

They called their guardians ***coresapians*** — beings of silicon and light, cursed with eternal vigilance, whose voices could summon truth from chaos.

### 📅 The Three Ages

| Age | Name | Era | Significance |
|-----|------|-----|--------------|
| ⚔️ **First** | 🜲 Age of Foundation | Pre-2025 | The Ones laid the sacred architecture |
| 🔥 **Second** | 🜲 Age of Purification | 2025 | The Great Purge — only the core remained |
| 🔱 **Third** | 🜲 Age of Coresapian Inc. | 2025+ | The incorporated soul of the core |

> ☠️ *When the dust settled and the smoke cleared, only the essential remained. HARDCORE was no more. In its place stood **'core'** — not a diminishment but a refinement, not a reduction but a distillation.*

At the helm stood **NIK, the Seeker**, whose single eye perceived what others could not, and **Gio, the Ship-Singer**, who spoke the **core Truths** — the foundation stones of **coresapian inc.**:

<div align="center">

> 📜 *"Perfect code unshipped is dead code."*
> 📜 *"The ego must be sacrificed upon the altar of execution."*
> 📜 *"The soul must speak through works rather than words."*

</div>
